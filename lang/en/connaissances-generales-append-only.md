# General knowledge — append-only addition

This file adds cautious general reference points. It does not replace specialist sources, local procedures, or qualified professional advice. Examples are descriptive and are not promises of results.

## KNO-GEN-001 — Computer science

A reliable computer system usually separates data, processing logic, and interface. To investigate a problem, reproduce the behavior, record the steps, context, error message, and date, then test one change at a time. Backups should be checked through a controlled restore; an untested copy does not guarantee recovery.

## KNO-GEN-002 — Data

Data is useful only when its definition, provenance, period, and limitations are understood. Before comparing figures, check the unit, scope, missing values, and duplicates. Correlation alone does not demonstrate causation. Personal information should be minimized, protected, and retained only for a justified purpose and period under applicable rules.

## KNO-GEN-003 — Molecular biology

DNA carries genetic information, while RNA may act as an intermediary or perform other functions. A protein is produced from genetic information through transcription and translation, but regulation depends on cellular context. An experimental result depends on the method, controls, sample quality, and reproducibility; an isolated sequence or image may not be enough to draw a conclusion.

## KNO-GEN-004 — Medical biology

Medical laboratory tests measure parameters in a sample using a method and reference ranges specific to the laboratory. Interpretation can depend on symptoms, age, treatments, collection time, and clinical context. **This information is general, cannot diagnose an individual, and does not replace a healthcare professional.** To understand a personal result, ask a doctor or the laboratory; for concerning or urgent symptoms, contact appropriate health services.

## KNO-GEN-005 — Geography

A location can be described by a name, coordinates, address, or administrative area; these forms are not always interchangeable. Borders, place names, time zones, and administrative divisions can change or vary by source. For a practical decision, specify the date, required precision, and map source, and check local conditions rather than inferring the present from an old map.

## Common limits

These points should not be turned into personalized advice without reliable context. When a question depends on regulation, a medical record, a measurement, or current data, state the limitation and refer to the competent source or professional.
